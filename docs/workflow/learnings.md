# Workflow learnings

Evidence behind the method of `docs/workflow/`, and what each stage teaches. Not required reading to run a stage: the briefs hold the conclusions. Read it when revising the method; an entry is added when the observation is made (`docs/workflow/README.md` §7). Entries are dated records: their references are those of their date.

Each entry states its basis:
- `evidence`: observed in a run or in the repository, with where;
- `docs`: from a tool's documentation, with page and date;
- `analysis`: reasoning not yet confirmed, with the measurement that would confirm it.

Ids `W<n>`, stable.
