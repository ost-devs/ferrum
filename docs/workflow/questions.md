# Open questions about the method

Questions for the consultant about how the work runs (`docs/workflow/README.md` §7), in the shape of `CLAUDE.md` "Raising a question", ids `M<n>` (the next is M25). The orchestrator adds one when an observation calls the method into question or a stop-loss fires; the consultant removes it when it records the decision (`docs/decisions/method.md`).

## M23. Conformance step 0 passed its stop-loss line on a budget no earlier run measured
- **Found.** The `test-writer` of step 0 (`docs/parity/ferrum-state/plan.md` "Conformance", step 0; card `docs/parity/ferrum-state/cards/test-writer.md` "Conformance step 0") used 55,034 tokens (`subagent_tokens` of its completion notice) against a budget of 30,000 and a stop line of 45,000 (`docs/workflow/README.md` §5 "Stop-loss"). It finished in one attempt; `control all`, `control 1` and `handoff` pass at its commit c08e6da (ledger, 2026-11-24). The orchestrator could not stop it at the line: the run returned once, at its end (`docs/workflow/learnings.md` W51).
- **Open because.** The plan sets 30,000 for this step and the stop-loss rule asks for a method question when a step passes half as much again; neither says how a budget for a change to the replayer's core is derived. The earlier rounds on the replayer (`cards/test-writer.md`, "After U4" 60,000, "the last round" 90,000, "a round after Q120" 30,000) were each given a budget of their own; the ledger rows of those rounds hold what they used.
- **Bears on it.** The plan's remaining budgets for this stage are step 2 (150,000), step 3 (5 × 210,000) and step 4 (150,000), each with its own stop line. The stop-loss of a worker that returns only at its end fires after the tokens are spent: the line marks the overrun, it does not cut it. Wall time is not tokens: the worker waited 1,123 s on one tool run. Deduction, not measured: the worker's context holds the files it read whole (the docstring of `tools/impl_check.py` is about 80 lines; `driver.rs` about 740).
- **Blocks.** Nothing: the step is done. The budgets of steps 2 to 4 are set before their cards are written.
- **Raised by.** The orchestrator, 2026-11-24.

## M24. Step 2 of the conformance plan asks a `test-writer` to write where its definition's write guard refuses
- **Found.** `docs/parity/ferrum-state/plan.md` "Conformance" step 2 gives G8 to a `test-writer` (budget 150,000): benchmarks "in `crates/ferrum-state/benches/`", "the same inputs timed on cm6 by a script beside `oracle/cm6/`". The definition `.claude/agents/test-writer.md` runs `.claude/hooks/guard.py only-write=crates/*/tests/** only-write=.scratch/**` on every Write and Edit, so neither path can be written by that agent. A bench target also needs a `[[bench]]` entry in `crates/ferrum-state/Cargo.toml` (outside both paths), and, if a harness crate is used, a dev-dependency: the crate's `[dev-dependencies]` hold `ferrum-state` itself and `serde_core` only, and `design.md` §1 says of dependencies "No other" (it names `serde_json`). `rust-crates.md` Code 6 asks the maintainer only for a direct dependency not licensed MIT or Apache-2.0.
- **Open because.** The plan names the role and the paths; the guard is part of the role's definition (`docs/workflow/README.md` §8: a write guard covers Write and Edit, not a shell command). Which of the three gives way, and whether G8 timings use a harness crate or std's clock, the plan does not say.
- **Bears on it.** `python3 tools/conf_check.py ferrum-state costs` sorts the 953 behaviors by their `cost` text: 499 local, 64 logarithmic, 217 linear or more, 173 that no pattern settles (`docs/parity/ferrum-state/conformance/costs.jsonl`); the local and logarithmic ones belong to 236 items, which is the size of the step. The timings are best taken when nothing else runs on the machine; mutation is running now and will for hours (1,316 mutants, `-j 2`).
- **Blocks.** Step 2 (G8), from its card. Steps 3 and 4 do not wait on it, but step 3 waits on mutation's survivors.
- **Raised by.** The orchestrator, 2026-11-24.
