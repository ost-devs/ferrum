## M30. Step 4's budget and its sample were set before step 3's costs were measured, and they do not fit each other
- **Found.** `docs/parity/ferrum-state/plan.md` step 4 gives the sixth verifier 150,000 to re-check 10% of the verdicts, at least 20, re-running their reproductions, drawn per run of step 3 with each run's lines at least in their share. `python3 tools/conf_check.py ferrum-state recheck --seed 20261126` (written for this step, `tools/README.md`) gives each of the 1,627 verdict lines of `raw/conformance-*.jsonl` to the commit that first wrote it: 24 runs, and a tenth of each is 171 lines (70 hazard, 25 item, 21 test, 18 sentinel, 14 shared, 9 mutant, 9 branch, 5 panic); `.scratch/conformance/ferrum-state/recheck-sample.jsonl`. Learning W56.
- **Open because.** The 150,000 dates from 2026-11-19 (plan "Expected cost"), before M27 to M29 fitted the cost of a run to what it reads and writes; no rule in `docs/workflow/README.md` §5 "Budgets" covers a re-check. The gaps rule counts about 4,000 per mutant with its probe and 3,000 per outcome; the hazards rule 330 per line written beside the files read; the specification stage's three re-check samples (W25) cost 250,000 to 330,000 for 28 items each. The plan names the six runs that judged without the rules of `crates/**` (M28) as the reason to draw per run, which `recheck` does; it does not say what a re-check of a line of each kind must re-run (a `SAFE` shared line or an `EXERCISED` hazard has a repro of another form than a mutant's probe).
- **Bears on it.** Deduction: by the gaps rule, 171 re-checks are about 25,000 + 27,000 + 171 × 3,000 to 4,000, 0.56 to 0.73 million, four to five times the budget; the M29 cut (about 240,000 per run) would make it three runs. The commit 60726ac and 1d41c6e are one run cut by the usage limit and resumed, counted by the tool as two. E58 waits on this step: it compares the overturn share of verdicts from runs that ended above and below 200,000 (eight verifier runs ended above, all hazards runs: U1's other groups, U2's `ChangeDesc` group, U3's other groups and lists, U4a's two, U4b's H1 to H3).
- **Blocks.** Step 4, the sixth verifier, which is the next step of the stage; then step 5.
