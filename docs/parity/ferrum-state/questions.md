## Q137. A hazard that cannot arise with Ferrum's types has no verdict in the verifier card
- **Found.** The card `docs/parity/ferrum-state/cards/verifier.md` (M27) gives a `hazard` line the verdicts `EXERCISED` (with its check) or `FINDING`. The U1 hazards run for `Text` and `Line` wrote 36 of its 84 lines as `UNREACHABLE`: about 30 because the hazard's input is outside what Ferrum's types can express (negative, fractional or NaN positions and line numbers under `usize`, a `dir` other than plus or minus one under `IterDirection`; D12, Q9, N11, `state.cm6.239.d1`; the fixtures that hold such input are left out with reason `type`), and the rest because cm6 itself cannot observe the hazard (`Line` fields `readonly` at `mirrors/cm6/state/src/text.ts:566-574`; `Line.text` aliasing of an immutable string). Example: `state.Line.B1:h1` in `docs/parity/ferrum-state/raw/conformance-text.jsonl`. Where part of such a hazard can be written in Ferrum's types, the run traced that part to compared fixtures.
- **Open because.** `UNREACHABLE` is a verdict of the card's lists (branch outcomes, lines), not of hazards; neither `EXERCISED` nor `FINDING` states "no input of Ferrum's types reaches this", and the check `conf_check hazards --verdicts` counts lines by id, not by verdict.
- **Bears on it.** The next hazards runs (U1's other groups, then U2 to U4b) meet the same kind: every hazard of the form "JS accepts X" where Ferrum's signature excludes X. The orchestrator tells them to write such a hazard as the first run did (`UNREACHABLE` with its reason) until this is decided, so the lines can be relabelled mechanically.
- **Blocks.** Nothing now; the verdict of G7 on hazards (step 5).
- **Raised by.** The orchestrator, 2026-11-24, from the U1 hazards run.

## Q138. A ChangeDesc read from JSON can have a total length that no isize holds
- **Found.** `ChangeDesc::from_json` accepts each number below 2^53 (`json_length`, `crates/ferrum-state/src/change.rs:773-780`; row `state.cm6.160.d1`). 1,025 pairs `[9007199254740991, -1]` are accepted, and `length()` (`:72`) and `new_length()` (`:79`) overflow `isize`: a panic "attempt to add with overflow" in a debug build (the U2 verifier's probe, `.scratch/verify-change/hz-probe`), a wrap in release. The same sum feeds `iter_gaps`, `iter_changes`, `try_map_pos` and `touches_range`. cm6 adds floats and returns about 9.23e18 (a deduction from `mirrors/cm6/state/src/change.ts:34-48`, not run).
- **Open because.** D12 excludes negative, fractional, NaN and infinite numbers, and D13 has `from_json` return `Err` for those; every number here is in the domain, only their sum is not. No row of `raw/decisions.jsonl` (`state.cm6.160.d1`, `state.cm6.156.d2`) and no section of `design.md` covers a total.
- **Bears on it.** No document can be this long; the input reaches Ferrum only through `from_json`, that is outside data (D13). cm6's own result is past 2^53, so imprecise there too. A release build returns a wrapped `usize` with no error. The same run found ten more sentinel findings of one shape, `as isize` on a `usize` above `isize::MAX` (`raw/conformance-change.jsonl`, kind `sentinel`, `src/change.rs:207`, `:214`, `:244`, `:515`, `:529`, `:617`, `:643`, `:715`, `:718`), as U1's run did at `src/text.rs:1051-1052` (item `state.cm6.240`, NONCONFORMANT): those are findings for the round of step 5, this one is open because the domain of a sum is not stated.
- **Blocks.** The item verdicts of `state.cm6.149` (`length`) and `state.cm6.150` (`newLength`), `UNVERIFIED` in step 3; nothing before step 5.
- **Raised by.** The U2 verifier (hazards run), 2026-11-24; queued by the orchestrator.

## Q139. Ferrum aborts on deep layer recursion where cm6 throws an error that can be caught
- **Found.** Ferrum recurses once per layer in `update_inner` (`crates/ferrum-state/src/rangeset.rs:665-670`), `map` (`:711`), `RangeSetBuilder::finish_inner` (`:1103-1105`) and in the drop of the `Arc<RangeSetInner>` chain (no `Drop` impl); cm6 recurses in `update` (`mirrors/cm6/state/src/rangeset.ts:298-299`), `map` (`:330`), `addRange`, `finishInner`, `size` and `length`. The U3 verifier's probe (`.scratch/verify-rangeset/depth`, n nested ranges `[i, 2n-i]`, one layer each, on the 8 MiB main thread): cm6 under node throws `RangeError` from `update` at 5,000 layers, completes `map` at 5,000, throws from `of` at 10,000; Ferrum's debug build aborts with a stack overflow in `map` at 5,000; its release build completes at 10,000 and aborts at 20,000 (hazard `state.RangeSet.update.B9:h1`, `FINDING`, in `raw/conformance-rangeset.jsonl`).
- **Open because.** The rows `state.cm6.183.d1`, `state.cm6.184.d1` and `state.cm6.184.d2` and `design.md` settle neither the depth a set may reach nor how a too deep one fails; no check exercises it.
- **Bears on it.** cm6's failure is an exception a caller can catch; Ferrum's aborts the process. Both fail only at thousands of nested layers, which nested ranges each needing a layer of their own produce. A debug build fails earlier than cm6, a release build later. A spawned Rust thread has a 2 MiB stack by default, so it would fail earlier still (a deduction, not measured).
- **Blocks.** The verdict of hazard `state.RangeSet.update.B9:h1` at step 5; nothing before.
- **Raised by.** The U3 verifier (hazards run), 2026-11-26; queued by the orchestrator.

## Q140. A caller's bound at or above 2^63 changes what `RangeSet`'s queries return, and no decision says whether the domain row covers query bounds
- **Found.** `between`, `update` (`filter_to`, `filter_from`), `RangeSet::eq` (`from`, `to`), `spans`, `iter`, `iter_sets`, `goto` and the `min_point_size` of `compare` and `spans` cast the caller's `usize` with `as i64` (`crates/ferrum-state/src/rangeset.rs:200`, `:610`, `:613`, `:738`, `:760`, `:771`, `:787`, `:820`, `:830`, `:849`, `:850`). With `usize::MAX`: `between(0, usize::MAX, f)` makes no call; `update` with `filter_to: Some(usize::MAX)` never calls its filter and keeps every range; `RangeSet::eq(a, b, 0, usize::MAX)` returns true for two sets that differ at `[4,6]`; a cursor starts at the first range where cm6 ends; `min_point_size: Some(usize::MAX)` compares or spans every range. From 1e9 to `i64::MAX` the results are cm6's. The U3 verifier's probe: `.scratch/verify-rangeset/hz`; its lines in `raw/conformance-rangeset.jsonl` (kind `sentinel`, 24 findings, `:610`, `:738` and `:830` high).
- **Open because.** `state.cm6.187.d1` (reproduce) says positions at or beyond 1e9 are outside `RangeSet`'s domain; it is not a D11 deviation and states no failure, and the limit is written only in the module doc (`src/rangeset.rs:4-5`). D13 asks for a panic or cm6's result, and D12 does not exclude these values. Whether the row's "positions" include a query's bounds, which no range stores, nothing says.
- **Bears on it.** In cm6, `between`, `update` and `iter` only compare a bound, so they give their result for any number (`mirrors/cm6/state/src/rangeset.ts:286`, `:290`, `:343`, `:606-614`); `eq` of equal sets and `spans` with `to` at or above 1e9 never return in cm6 (`:405`, `:437`, deduced), and Ferrum's `spans` does not return either (probe, timed out at 5 s). `usize::MAX` is how Rust callers commonly write "to the end". U2's `src/change.rs:643` (a change filter returning `(0, usize::MAX)` blocks nothing, high) and Q138 have the same shape; U1's `state.cm6.240` (`src/text.rs:1051-1052`) too. Stored positions at or above 2^63 also wrap (`:1038` to `:1060`): those are inside the domain row, and no document reaches them.
- **Blocks.** Whether the three high sentinel findings (`:610`, `:738`, `:830`) stand, and with them the `CONFORMANT` lines of `state.cm6.183`, `.185`, `.189` and `.196`; step 5's G7 verdict.
- **Raised by.** The U3 verifier (hazards run), 2026-11-26; queued by the orchestrator.
