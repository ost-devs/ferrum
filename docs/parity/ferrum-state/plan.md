# ferrum-state: plan

How `ferrum-state` goes through each stage (`docs/workflow/README.md` §1), written by the consultant from the crate as it stands and from the ledger. The orchestrator follows it and writes the cards from it. The record of each choice here: `docs/decisions/ferrum-state.md`.

## Specification: what remains (written 2026-11-13)

What exists, checked by `python3 tools/spec_check.py ferrum-state` at the commit that added this file: 264 inventory rows (261 `CORE`, 3 `DROP`), 950 behaviors, 172 cm6 test cases, 4,950 fixtures in 5 chunks. Every generator reproduces its fixtures byte for byte in the order of `oracle/cm6/order.json`, and the recorded runs reach every line of `@codemirror/state` 6.7.6 but 9 and every branch but 24 (`raw/coverage.jsonl`, 33 sites). The six verification samples are closed (`verification.md`); their rates are in the ledger. The behavior rows keep their `deps`, `hazards`, `stack` and `consumer_values` fields: they are the evidence the questions Q41–Q44 and the design cite.

These steps remain, in this order. A step starts when the one before it is committed, except where a step says otherwise, and C may start while A's later units run.

**A. Fixture inputs become programs.** Fixtures that record their call as JavaScript text for a reader (`raw/fixture-calls.jsonl`) cannot be run by a Rust replayer: the implementation would translate each call text into Rust by hand, with nothing to show a wrong translation but the output it then fails to match, and conformance could not run fresh seeds through the crate. Chunks whose inputs were already data needed none of the input corrections of the samples (`docs/workflow/learnings.md` W39).

Done: the one encoding in `tools/cm6/oracle-lib.ts` (c2d1fca), and the pilot, chunk `rangeset` (8cb893a): 388 of 388 fixtures, one `spec-auditor` at `medium`, 194,385 tokens against 200,000, the check passed at its first run. What the pilot changed in this step (record: `docs/decisions/ferrum-state.md`, "Step A after the pilot"):
- An input is a small program (`{values, steps, out}`: ops, variables, a conditional, `try`), not one op per fixture: that is how 94 call texts became about 75 ops. The remaining chunks write their inputs in the same language, taken out of `rangeset` into the tool, so that the replayer implements one evaluator, not one dialect per chunk (`docs/workflow/specification.md` §5 item 3).
- What shows that an input is complete is replay with read tracking, not a list of keys that may hold text: the list missed 27 `rangeset` fixtures with prose under other keys.
- Each op states what it observes (its kinds), so that S5 decides per op what Ferrum's replayer does with it: 177 of the 388 `rangeset` fixtures run an op that reads cm6's internal layout or object identity (`set.dump`, which records the chunk layout, in 139).

Units:
1. **A1, the tool** (orchestrator, before any worker of step A starts):
   - `tools/cm6/program.ts`: the language, taken out of `oracle/cm6/rangeset/interp.ts` and documented once at its top: an input is a program `{steps, out, …}` or a bare expression `{"op": …, …}` (a program of one step whose value is the output, the form of chunk `change`); `as` and `{"$": name}`, `if`, `try` and its modes, the `out` template, the ops that touch no cm6 value (`same`, `all`, `not`, `count`, `slice`, `item`, `column`, `allSame`, `sameItems`, `copy`, `lengths`, `jsonEqual`, `object`), and `map {over: <expr>, as: <name>, do: <expr>}` for a call repeated over a list. `run` decodes the input, then evaluates it; a chunk adds its own setup (for `rangeset`, `values`) and its ops.
   - An op registry: a chunk registers each op with its kinds, one or more of `api` (calls or reads a member cm6 exports and documents), `internal` (reads, replaces or depends on a member cm6 marks `@internal` or does not export, or on its internal layout), `identity` (its result depends on JavaScript object identity), `callbacks` (it observes how often or in which order cm6 calls a function the input supplies, beyond what the call returns), `harness` (no cm6 value touched). Each interpreter exports its descriptive keys (keys no op reads: `seed`, `case`, `note`, …).
   - The evaluator reads every part of a callback spec when it builds the callback, and marks as read what it skips: a step whose `if` is false, the steps and `out` after an uncaught throw, the `result` of a `try` that threw.
   - Replay in `tools/spec_check.py` (through a script it runs, `tools/cm6/replay.ts`), for every fixture of each chunk: decode the recorded input, wrap it so that every key the interpreter or cm6 reads is recorded, run it, encode the output and compare it with the recorded one; a fixture whose output is `{"timeout": ms}` replays through `runIsolated` with that limit. It prints per chunk, and fails on any non-zero count: fixtures that differ; keys never read that are not descriptive (by path, with counts); descriptive keys holding call text (the pattern of `js_text`); `out` templates with a literal leaf; fixtures that run no op of kind `api` or `internal`; ops without kinds, or not named in the interpreter's header; `eval` or `Function` in the chunk's files. It writes `raw/fixture-ops.jsonl`: per chunk and op, its kinds, the number of fixtures that ran it, and their behavior ids.
   - The harness `tsconfig.json` without `baseUrl` (TypeScript 7.0.2, the global `tsc`, rejects it; every path is absolute): without it `tsc` type-checks `oracle/cm6/rangeset/interp.ts` with no error (tried 2026-11-13). The command goes in `tools/README.md` and the card; it is not a gate.
   - `oracle/cm6/rangeset/interp.ts` imports the language; `oracle/cm6/rangeset/interp-cli.ts` and `oracle/cm6/facet/interp-cli.ts` are removed (replay replaces them; `oracle/cm6/change/interp-cli.ts` stays, it is the isolated runner of the `change` generators). `tools/README.md` and the docstring of `tools/spec_check.py` say what each new line means.
   - Check: `python3 tools/spec_check.py ferrum-state --chunk rangeset --same-outputs-as c2d1fca` passes but for the kinds of `rangeset`'s ops (A2 gives them): 388 fixtures replay equal, no unread key. A prototype of the read tracking replayed all 388 `rangeset` fixtures equal and found 20 groups of unread keys, every one a skipped step, a step after a throw, or a callback spec cm6 never called: what the evaluator's marking removes.
2. **A2, `rangeset` kinds and chunk `change`** (one `spec-auditor`; the pilot worker resumed if it can be, for `rangeset`). `rangeset`: register its ops with their kinds. `change`: its inputs are already bare expressions; its interpreter registers its ops with kinds in the language, reads its input with `decode`, and drops its own conventions (`num`, `args`, `deepNum`, `clean`, the `{"num": …}` and `null` rules of its header); its generators write JavaScript values (17 inputs hold `{"num": …}`; 29 do not run as recorded at c2d1fca). Budget 100,000.
3. **A3 `text`** (budget 400,000) and **A4 `facet`** (budget 200,000), each one `spec-auditor`, in parallel with A2 once A1 is committed. `text`: every one of its 1,656 fixtures becomes a program, those whose input already names an `op` included. `facet`: the scenario and `combineConfig` inputs become bare expressions of the language (`{"op": "scenario", …}`), its conventions (`"<undefined>"`, `{"num": …}`, `"<function>"`) give way to the one encoding, and the 135 `code` fixtures become programs.
4. **A5 `state`** (budget 550,000), after A4 is merged: an extension in a `state` input is written in the vocabulary of `oracle/cm6/facet/interp.ts` (its `defs`, Node and getter forms), which `state`'s interpreter imports, so that the replayer builds extensions one way. The worker may add a form to `oracle/cm6/facet/interp.ts` and nothing else there; the check then runs on `facet` too.

For every unit:
- Effort `medium`: every error of this step shows in the check before merge.
- Check, run by the worker until it passes and by the orchestrator before merging: `python3 tools/spec_check.py ferrum-state --chunk <chunk> --same-outputs-as c2d1fca` passes (same ids, items, behaviors and outputs; regeneration byte for byte; every replay line at zero). The list of inputs recorded as text (`raw/fixture-calls.jsonl`) is the worker's list of what remains, not the check; when A ends, the orchestrator removes it and its count from the tool.
- The interpreter's header marks `(pos)` each argument and output field that is a position, an offset or a length in UTF-16 code units of a document the input holds, so that the replayer maps them to the unit of D1 (UTF-8 bytes) without reading the generators (`docs/workflow/implementation.md` §5).
- Card (`docs/parity/ferrum-state/cards/spec-auditor.md`): write only `oracle/cm6/<chunk>/` (A5 also the additions to `oracle/cm6/facet/interp.ts`), `fixtures/<chunk>.jsonl`, `.scratch/<chunk>/`. Give the worker `tools/cm6/program.ts` as the language and `oracle/cm6/rangeset/interp.ts` as the model of a chunk's ops; what each line of the check means, so that the worker does not read the tool; the kinds and what S5 does with them; the purpose: Ferrum's replayer runs the same input, op by op, from the header alone. A construct no chunk op can express and the language lacks is reported, not written into `tools/`.
- Outputs keep their values, not their wrapping (Q58, `docs/decisions/ferrum-state.md`): the language writes every output as `{"ok": value}` or `{"error": …}`, so an output that c2d1fca held bare is now exactly `{"ok": <it>}`; the check counts these ("now wrapped in ok") and accepts nothing else. A value written in the input reaches an output only through the language's op `label {text}` (a word an old probe wrote to say which step was reached); a variable bound by a chunk's `data` step is never referenced by `out`, which the check counts and fails on. `data` is for values the program hands to cm6.
- Stop-loss as `docs/workflow/README.md` §5: at 1.5 times the budget, or at a third failing run of the check on the same fixtures.

**B. The sites no run reaches.** For each of the 33 sites of `raw/coverage.jsonl`, one `spec-auditor` (effort `high`: whether an input reaches a branch is a reading nobody checks) reads the function and records in `verification.md`, under "Coverage": a fixture added through the chunk's interpreter with its behavior row, or the reason no input reaches the site, or that the site belongs to a `DROP` item (`fromCodePoint`, `mirrors/cm6/state/src/char.ts:31`) or is an artifact of mapping compiled code to source (a site on a declaration or closing line). Runs after A so that new fixtures are programs. Budget 150,000. Check: `tools/spec_check.py` again; every site has an entry.

**C. S5 Decision** (`docs/workflow/specification.md` §9), by the consultant, in three calls.

Done at the first call (record: `docs/decisions/ferrum-state.md`, "S5, first part"): 45 questions decided (`raw/decisions.jsonl`, `.claude/rules/decisions.md` D11–D15); the naming entries N6–N10, which settle every row of `raw/names.jsonl`; `design.md`; the first iteration of D1, D2, D5 and D6, confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-11-13 (`docs/decisions/foundation.md`), which closed Q8, Q41–Q44 and Q54. The input classes JavaScript's lack of types allows are excluded once, by D12.

Second call, when step A ends (`raw/fixture-ops.jsonl` complete, every input a program): what the replayer does with each op that is not only `api` (run it against Ferrum's counterpart under the feature `__test`, compare a projection of its output, or leave its behaviors to a named check); the fixture lists of the rows of `raw/decisions.jsonl` whose `fixtures_to_list` is true. For it the orchestrator gathers, with a tool and not by reading: per chunk and op with a kind other than `api`, the output fields that hold an identity or a layout value; and for each row with `fixtures_to_list`, the fixtures whose input matches the row's condition (the row states it).

Third call, when step B ends: the "Implementation" section of this plan, and the approval, which states the residual risk the samples measured (`docs/workflow/learnings.md` W42). D1, D2, D5 and D6 are in force (`.claude/rules/decisions.md`).

Then S6 (the end-of-stage review and the report for the maintainer).

## Improvement delta

Runs after the conformance verdict is accepted and before the S0 of `ferrum-view` (`docs/workflow/README.md` §1 "When an improvement is made"). Its procedure is written by the consultant when the verdict is accepted, from `raw/improvements.jsonl` as it then stands. What is fixed now:
- `state.imp.001` (grapheme clusters by UAX #29) is made there, first, as the pilot of the mechanism: the patch in `oracle/improvements/state.imp.001/`, the tool that writes `fixtures/improved/state.imp.001.jsonl` and fails on a change outside the row's behaviors, tests from those values, then the code.
- Every row whose `when` names this delta is settled there: adopted and made, scheduled for a later named point, or declined with its reason.
- Before it starts, a `rust-researcher` and a `cm6-researcher` gather what each row's `note` says is not yet known.
